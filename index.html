Church creative direction in ChatGPT

Sermon Graphics

Premium cinematic sermon artwork built as three separate, matching renders for church screens, YouTube and scripture presentation.

What it creates

  • A purpose-built 3:1 main service graphic
  • A separate 16:9 YouTube thumbnail
  • A separate darker, textless 16:9 scripture background

Each format is independently composed rather than cropped from another image.

How it works

Provide a sermon title and preacher name, or upload a sermon document. You can also add creative direction such as mood, palette, imagery or style. Sermon Graphics develops one coherent visual campaign and renders each required format separately.
