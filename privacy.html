Sermon Graphics Privacy Terms Support

Effective 6 October 2026

Privacy Policy

This policy explains the data practices of Sermon Graphics, a skills-only ChatGPT plugin published by Joseph Fresi.

Information used

When you use Sermon Graphics, you may provide sermon titles, preacher names, creative direction, sermon documents, reference images and other material in your ChatGPT conversation. Sermon Graphics uses that material only to carry out the creative workflow you request.

How information is processed

Sermon Graphics does not operate its own external server, user database or analytics system. The plugin relies on native ChatGPT capabilities. Content you provide in ChatGPT is processed by OpenAI under the terms and privacy settings that apply to your ChatGPT account and service.

Information received by the publisher

Joseph Fresi does not automatically receive a copy of your ChatGPT prompts, sermon files or generated images simply because you use Sermon Graphics.

If you contact support by email, the information you choose to include in that email is received by Joseph Fresi and may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain support records and resolve related issues.

Sharing and sale of data

Sermon Graphics does not sell personal information and does not use an independent advertising or data-broker system. The plugin does not offer payments or purchases.

Retention and deletion

Sermon Graphics does not maintain a separate copy of your ChatGPT conversation or uploaded sermon content. Retention of content within ChatGPT is controlled by OpenAI and your ChatGPT account settings. To request deletion of information you sent directly by support email, contact fresijoe@gmail.com.

Security and content choices

Do not provide confidential, private or copyrighted material unless you are authorised to use it. You are responsible for deciding what sermon or reference material you submit.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the plugin changes. Material changes should be reflected on this page.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to fresijoe@gmail.com.
